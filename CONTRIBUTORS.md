# Project lineage and contributors

FIDB Factory continues the original `SegmondFault/FIDB-gen-POC` project.

The current history incorporates substantial development from the
`rdmmf/FIDB-gen-POC` fork, including the `unify-recipes-malware-support`
branch. Later work expanded that foundation into the present build,
validation, scheduling, retention and export system.

The Git commit history is the authoritative record of individual authorship
and preserves the attribution of the original project, the incorporated fork
and subsequent development.
